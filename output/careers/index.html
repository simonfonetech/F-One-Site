Discover Your Career with F One.

At F One, we’re always on the lookout for motivated, down-to-earth people who care about doing a great job for customers. We’re a growing, family-run technology business where everyone plays a part, ideas are welcomed, and hard work is genuinely recognised. If you’re looking for a role where you can develop your skills, feel supported, and enjoy coming to work, we’d love to hear from you.

Get in touch

Why Join F One.

We believe people do their best work when they feel valued, trusted, and supported. That’s why we focus heavily on building a positive, inclusive working environment where you’re treated as an individual, not just a job title. We invest in training, encourage progression, and give you the tools and backing you need to grow your career with us, not just do a job.

Get in touch

Our Office.

Our Southampton office is a modern, comfortable workspace designed to support how people actually work day to day. With up-to-date technology, breakout areas, and great transport links nearby, it’s an easy and enjoyable place to spend your working week. We believe a good environment makes a real difference.

Explore Our Roles.

Our team continues to grow, and we regularly have opportunities across different areas of the business. Whether you’re customer-focused, technically minded, or thrive in a fast-paced sales environment, there’s a place for the right people at F One.

If you enjoy building relationships and are motivated by performance and progression, our sales roles offer strong earning potential alongside structured support and development. You’ll work with businesses to genuinely improve how they operate, not just push products.

Our admin and operations roles are the backbone of the business. From customer support to accounts and internal coordination, these positions suit organised, reliable people who enjoy keeping things running smoothly and being part of a close-knit office team.

If you have experience with phone systems, networks, or WiFi, our engineering roles give you the opportunity to work with modern technology and real customers. You’ll be supported by a knowledgeable team and encouraged to develop your skills as the business grows.

What It’s Like to Work Here.

We’re proud of the culture we’ve built at F One. We work hard, support each other, and celebrate successes together. People are approachable, teamwork is encouraged, and no one is left to struggle on their own. It’s a place where effort is noticed and progression is achievable.

"Working at F One has been amazing. The team is tight-knit, supportive, and every day brings something new."

Lucy Short, Solution Coordinator

"Thanks to the opportunities here, I’ve earned far more than just my basic pay. I’ve just bought my first home!"

James Stacey, Telemarketing Team Manager

Bringing the Team Together.

Staying connected as a team matters to us. Throughout the year, we organise a range of social and team-building activities, from summer events and nights out to more relaxed get-togethers. These aren’t forced, but they’re there to help people get to know each other beyond their day-to-day roles.

Recognising Hard Work.

We believe good work should be recognised. Our staff rewards programme highlights individual and team achievements, with incentives ranging from vouchers to tech prizes and other rewards. It’s our way of saying thank you and making sure effort doesn’t go unnoticed.
