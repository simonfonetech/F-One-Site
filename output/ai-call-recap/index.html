AI Call Recap.

AI Call Recap turns every call into a valuable asset, with clear, AI-powered visibility into what was said, how it went and what matters most. Each recorded call gets a full transcription, sentiment analysis, topic detection and a concise summary, so your team stays engaged during the conversation and spends less time on follow-ups, while managers always have an accurate record for dispute resolution, coaching and analytics.

Get in touch

What You Get After Every Call.

Within minutes of a recorded call ending, a recap appears in the Elevate desktop and mobile apps and in the Archiving portal. No new app, no change to how your team works.

  • Transcription: a complete, searchable record of everything said during the call, so nothing is missed or misconstrued.
  • Summary: a concise recap highlighting follow-ups and takeaways, saving time on note-taking after the call.
  • Sentiment analysis: an instant read on the overall tone of the call, so you can quickly gauge how the conversation went.
  • Topic detection: key subjects identified and grouped, making the most important points easy to find and review.

With AI Call Recap, Your Business Can.

Save Time

  • Instead of writing up post-call notes, employees move straight on to the next call or task.

Improve Focus

  • Rather than trying to take notes during a call, employees can give the customer their full attention.

Ensure Accuracy

  • Managers get an accurate account of every call conversation, making disputes and follow-ups far easier.

Mitigate Risk

  • Managers and business owners can easily access stored recaps for better visibility into call conversations and insights.

Where It Makes a Difference.

  • Customer support teams: agents focus on the conversation, not the notes. When the call ends there is an accurate account of it and they can move straight on to the next customer. The outcome is a better experience for employee and customer alike.
  • Sales and prospecting calls: reps can ask detailed questions and concentrate on understanding the customer, then use the recap and transcript to prepare a tailored, accurate quote quickly. More wins from a faster turnaround and a right-first-time quote.
  • Compliance and dispute resolution: when a complaint is escalated, a manager can search Archiving for the recap of the original call and address the issue quickly, knowing the account is accurate.
Get in touch

How It Works.

After the call ends, the recording is automatically processed and a recap is generated within minutes, available in both the Elevate apps and the Archiving portal. Processing happens securely in Intermedia’s data centres and is engineered to comply with regional and national call recording standards. Recaps are stored in the Archiving platform, with its access controls, encryption and retention options, so your call records stay compliant with legal and regulatory requirements.

Find out more

What You Need.

AI Call Recap is included at no additional cost on Essentials, Pro and Enterprise licences (and their For Teams equivalents). A few things need to be in place, all of which we set up for you.

Call Recording

  • Call recording enabled at the account level.
  • On-demand: users record by pressing the record button in the app or dialling #9.
  • Automatic: hunt group or user-level calls recorded from the moment the call starts.

Recap & Archiving

  • AI Call Recap enabled at the user or hunt group level.
  • Archiving enabled, with 30-day retention included or annual retention options for longer.
