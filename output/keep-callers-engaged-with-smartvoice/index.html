Keep Callers Engaged with SmartVoice

Let’s be honest, nobody enjoys being put on hold. But those few seconds don’t have to be wasted time. They can actually be a great chance to share something useful with your customers, and that’s exactly what SmartVoice helps you do.

At F One Technologies, we’re always looking for ways to make our customers’ phone systems work harder for them. SmartVoice is our latest tool that makes it simple to create clear, professional on-hold messages that keep callers informed and engaged.

You can use it to update customers on new products, special offers, seasonal changes, or even just your opening hours, all while they’re waiting on the line. Every bit of communication counts, and SmartVoice turns that quiet time into an opportunity to connect.

What Makes SmartVoice Stand Out

SmartVoice isn’t your standard on-hold messaging setup. It’s powered by clever AI voice technology, so you don’t need to hire a voiceover artist or record anything yourself. You just type what you want to say, and SmartVoice turns it into a natural, lifelike message in minutes.

You’ll also have access to a library of over 8,000 background music tracks, so you can pick something that fits your brand perfectly, whether that’s calm and professional or upbeat and friendly.

Traditional on-hold systems can be a bit of a headache, they take time, cost more than you expect, and often need someone else to update them. SmartVoice puts you back in control. You can create, edit, and manage your messages whenever you need to, without the hassle.

Simple. Smart. Affordable.

Need to let customers know about a bank holiday closure? Launching something new? Updating your hours? SmartVoice makes it quick and easy. You can make changes in minutes and keep your phone system sounding fresh all year round.

It’s just £9.99 a month, less than the cost of a few coffees, and gives you everything you need to keep your callers informed and your business sounding professional.

We built SmartVoice to be straightforward and affordable for any business, big or small. It’s a simple way to make a great first impression every time someone calls.

At F One, we’re all about helping our customers get more from their communications. SmartVoice is another step towards that, giving you the tools to stay connected, sound professional, and make every moment count. Find out more here: SmartVoice Messaging
