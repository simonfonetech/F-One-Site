Dental Practices.

Our Elevate phone system helps dental practices manage patient calls more smoothly. Calls are answered in order, missed calls can offer a callback, and recordings support training and peace of mind. Clear call reports show how busy reception really is, while SmartVoice on hold messages promote services like teeth whitening or Invisalign.

Find out more

Care Homes.

We help care homes stay connected with reliable WiFi, cordless phones, and easy to use phone systems. Using Elevate and business grade WiFi, staff can make and receive calls anywhere on site, stay reachable at all times, and avoid missed or dropped calls. Everything is designed to be dependable, simple to use, and easy for staff to rely on day to day.

Find out more

Estate Agencies.

Our Elevate phone system helps estate agents stay responsive and organised. Calls can be tracked so you can see how your team is performing, while call recordings help with training and resolving disputes. With mobile and computer apps, staff can take business calls from wherever, and SmartVoice keeps callers informed while they wait.

Find out more

Mobile & PC Apps.

Use your business number on your mobile, laptop or PC, wherever you are working. You can make and receive calls, listen to voicemails, join meetings and access contacts whether that’s in the office, at home or out with customers. It keeps your team connected and ensures you always present a consistent, professional presence.
