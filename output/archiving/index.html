Archiving.

Every call, message and meeting your business handles contains valuable information. Archiving automatically captures, retains and indexes your Elevate calls, SMS, chat, video meetings, Microsoft Teams and email in one secure, searchable place, so you can find any conversation in seconds and meet your governance and regulatory obligations with confidence.

Get in touch

What is Archiving?

Intelligent Archiving is designed to automatically capture, retain and simplify searching of your unified communications, contact centre, Microsoft Teams and email data. Every interaction is stored in a centralised, secure and encrypted location, giving you continuous historical access with retention periods ranging from 30 days to 10 years.

  • Archiving of phone calls, SMS, chat, video meetings, email and more.
  • Powerful contextual, item-level search to quickly find and preview relevant interactions.
  • Retention policies enforced automatically, with no administrative or user action after the initial activation.
  • Single store, single pane: all of your company’s communications in one platform.
Get in touch

Why Businesses Need Archiving.

Effective communication is crucial, and the volume of interactions across channels keeps growing. Archiving lets you safeguard those conversations and get real business value from them, addressing three key needs:

Productivity

  • Unified data insights: analyse conversations across every channel to uncover customer behaviour, employee performance and business trends, for better-informed decisions.
  • Coaching & quality assurance: managers can grade interactions, give feedback and lift frontline performance by reviewing employee and customer conversations in one place.
  • Knowledge & continuity: preserve and share communications so nothing is lost during staff turnover, leave or hand-overs, for faster resolutions and happier customers.

Mitigate Risk

  • Address disputes & complaints: a direct record of every conversation removes the he-said-she-said, protecting your reputation and resolving disputes quickly.
  • Satisfy legal obligations: with litigation, investigations and eDiscovery increasingly common, retained communications give courts, regulators and internal teams the evidence they need.

Compliance

  • Adhere to regulations: industry rules (HIPAA, FINRA, PCI-DSS, SEC and others) dictate how interactions must be recorded and preserved. Automated capture into tamper-proof storage keeps you compliant and helps avoid costly fines.

All Your Communications, One Platform.

Archiving integrates seamlessly with Elevate unified communications, contact centre, Microsoft Teams and email, and is designed to be deployed in minutes, giving you compliant retention of customer and employee communications without a separate system to manage.

  • Automatic preservation: captures and retains call records, voicemails, chats, SMS messages and emails.
  • Flexible retention: keep records for as long as your business case requires, from 30 days to 10 years.
  • Unlimited capacity: based on your chosen retention period, with no need to estimate storage requirements.
  • Personal access & Insights: every user can quickly access their own communications data and gain useful insights from it.
  • Data residency: complies with US, Canadian and European geographic location requirements.
Get in touch

Fast, Powerful Search.

Both content and metadata are indexed, so millions of items can be queried on dozens of properties in seconds. Search on text, chat names, participants, call duration, source, attachments and many more attributes, then preview the interaction right there in the results.

  • Keyword search & filters across every channel, with content summaries and previews.
  • eDiscovery & litigation support: apply legal hold to override retention periods, then manage and export all case documents.
  • Audit logs record every action carried out by users, including who viewed what, activities and sessions.
Get in touch

Secure & Compliant by Design.

Data is uploaded securely and encrypted in transit and at rest, with multi-factor authentication protecting access and export limited to authorised users. Identity and access management gives you full control over who can view and manage archived communications. Archiving supports HIPAA, FINRA and MiFID II compliance programmes, with optional WORM tamper-proof storage to satisfy SEC Rule 17a-4.

Get in touch

Add-On: Compliance Module.

The optional Compliance Module helps your organisation monitor communications for regulatory compliance, respond to litigation and prepare for investigations, cutting the time, cost and effort of supervision and case management.

Supervision & Monitoring

  • Compliance policies: create and back-test policies that automatically categorise spam or high-risk communications, reducing manual review time.
  • Lexicons: build keyword and phrase lists and attach them to policies.
  • Assign & escalate: assign documents to compliance team members and escalate for further review.

Legal Case Management

  • Case management: a complete workflow to identify, assign and manage communications for investigation, with automatic policy-based document addition.
  • Tags & filters: enrich and filter content for legal review, reducing volume and exporting only what is relevant.
  • Legal holds: preserve potentially relevant information with unlimited holds across all content sources, protecting it from deletion.
  • Export: quickly export documents in standards-based formats for use in legal review applications.

Up and Running in Minutes.

Archiving is designed to be deployed in minutes alongside your Elevate phone system. Once activated, your retention policies are enforced automatically with no ongoing administrative or user action, and our team sets everything up and supports it as part of your cloud phone system.
