Managed Switches.

Key Features.

TP Link Omada managed switches form the backbone of a reliable business network. They connect wired devices, supply power where needed, and give greater control over how data moves around your organisation.

Get in touch

Omada switches keep key devices such as phones, computers, servers, and WiFi access points connected consistently and securely.

Many Omada switches provide power directly to phones and access points, reducing extra plugs and keeping installations neat.

Important services like phone systems can be prioritised, helping ensure they always perform well.

Switches are managed alongside routers and WiFi through the Omada platform, making maintenance and troubleshooting simpler.
