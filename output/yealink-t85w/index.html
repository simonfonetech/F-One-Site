Yealink T85W.

Key Features.

The Yealink T85W is a premium desk phone designed for users who need more screen space, faster access to features, and a more modern calling experience. It’s ideal for managers, reception desks, and power users.

Get in touch

The spacious touchscreen makes navigating calls, contacts, and features quick and intuitive, reducing time spent searching through menus.

Clear voice performance helps maintain professional conversations, even during long discussions or important calls.

Wireless connectivity options allow flexible installation and support for compatible accessories, helping keep desks uncluttered.

Designed for users who rely heavily on their desk phone, the T85W offers speed, visibility, and control throughout the working day.
