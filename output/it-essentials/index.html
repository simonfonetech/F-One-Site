IT Essentials.

Our IT Essentials offering has been completely rebuilt around best in class security and management platforms. By partnering with Acronis and Uniqkey, we now deliver a joined up solution that protects your users, devices, data, and Microsoft 365 environment from modern cyber threats. Everything is centrally managed, continuously monitored, and backed by our UK based support team, giving you enterprise grade protection without enterprise complexity.

Licence Management.

We supply and manage Microsoft 365 user licences on your behalf, giving you a single point of contact for billing, support, and ongoing management. From adding and removing users to licence changes and security settings, we look after the day to day administration so your team gets the tools they need without the overhead of managing it internally.

Email Security.

Acronis Email Security protects your inbox from phishing emails, malicious links, and impersonation attempts. It works directly with Microsoft 365 to stop threats before they reach users, helping prevent account compromise, data loss, and costly mistakes caused by unsafe emails.

Find out more

Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR).

Acronis Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR) protects your devices from ransomware, viruses, and other cyber attacks. It continuously monitors how devices behave and automatically responds if something suspicious is detected. Our team reviews alerts and takes action on your behalf.

Find out more

Backup for Microsoft 365.

Acronis Backup for Microsoft 365 securely backs up your emails, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams data. If files are deleted, overwritten, or lost due to an attack, they can be restored quickly and easily. This ensures your business data is always protected.

Remote Monitoring & Management (RMM).

Acronis Remote Monitoring & Management (RMM) allows us to monitor the health, performance, and security of your devices in real time. Updates, fixes, and maintenance are handled remotely, often before users notice a problem. This reduces downtime and keeps systems reliable and up to date.

Security Awareness Training..

Acronis Security Awareness Training helps your team recognise real world cyber threats through short training sessions and simulated phishing emails. This reduces the risk of human error and supports security standards such as Cyber Essentials.

Password Manager.

Uniqkey provides a secure way for staff to store passwords and access business systems safely. It removes the need for weak or shared passwords, helping protect accounts while making it easier for users to log in securely.
