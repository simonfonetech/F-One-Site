Call Queues & Hunt Groups.

Make sure every call reaches the right people, even when the right people are busy. Hunt groups ring a team rather than an individual, an auto attendant guides callers to the right department, and call queues hold callers with music or messages until someone is free. For busier teams, advanced hunt groups add agent log-in, wrap-up time, wallboards, reports and supervisor tools, all configured for you and easy to adjust as the business changes.

Get in touch

Never Miss a Call.

Hunt groups are included on every Elevate plan and are the building block of good call handling: sales, support and reception each get a group, and calls ring the members of that group on their desk phones and apps until someone answers.

  • Hunt groups for each team or department, ringing members together or in turn.
  • Auto attendant menus (“press 1 for sales”) that route callers straight to the right group.
  • Opening hours, holidays and out-of-hours routing set once, so calls always go somewhere sensible: another group, a mobile or voicemail-to-email.
  • Overflow to a second group or a colleague when nobody in the first group can answer.
  • Voicemail to email with transcription, so a missed call is never a lost one.

Advanced Hunt Groups for Busy Teams.

When a team takes more calls than it can answer at once, advanced hunt groups on Pro and Enterprise plans turn a ring group into a proper call queue with the tools a small contact centre needs, without a separate contact centre system.

Queue Management

  • Call queuing with position announcements, hold music and greetings management.
  • Agent log-in and log-out so only people who are available receive calls.
  • Wrap-up time after each call, and configurable removal of agents who miss calls from the queue.
  • Smart greetings and additional call recording storage.

Supervision & Reporting

  • Wallboards showing calls waiting, agents available and answer times in real time.
  • Scheduled and graphical reports on call volumes, answer rates and missed calls by group and by agent.
  • Monitor, whisper and barge so supervisors can listen in, coach an agent privately or join a call.
  • Live View adds live sentiment and transcripts by queue for teams that need it.

See How Calls Are Handled.

Call history and analytics dashboards show how many calls each group receives, how quickly they are answered and where calls are being missed, so you can staff the busy periods, spot a problem before customers do and prove the service levels you are delivering.

  • Call history for every group and user, searchable by number, date and outcome.
  • Service adoption and quality dashboards that show how the system is being used and how calls are performing.
  • Call recording on group calls, with AI Call Recap summaries and searchable archives on eligible plans.

Set Up Around How You Work.

We design the call flow with you: which groups exist, who is in them, how long calls ring before overflowing, what callers hear while they wait and where calls go out of hours. It is all managed through a simple online portal, so adding a new starter to a group or changing the Christmas opening hours takes minutes and does not need an engineer.
