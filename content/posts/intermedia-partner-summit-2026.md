<p>This month, I will be attending the Intermedia International Partner Summit in Lisbon alongside one of our other Company Directors, Alex Viola, representing F One as a key Intermedia partner.</p>

<p>The summit is an invitation only event that brings together top performing partners from across the world. Senior leadership from Intermedia’s global business will also be there, including teams from the US, Europe and Asia, so it is one of the most important events in the partner calendar.</p>

<p>Taking place over three days from 15 to 17 April 2026, the summit gives us the opportunity to get a clear view of Intermedia’s product roadmap, wider strategy and upcoming developments. The agenda is built around strategy sessions, product deep dives and live demonstrations, along with direct access to their technical specialists and leadership team, which is always incredibly valuable from our side.</p>

<p>One of the most important parts of the event is the level of collaboration involved. Partners are encouraged to share feedback based on real customer experiences, helping shape how the platform evolves. That is something we really value, as it means we are not just working with new features once they are released, but actively contributing to how they are developed in the first place.</p>

<p>From our perspective, attending events like this is about staying close to the direction of the platform. It allows us to bring new features and improvements to our customers more quickly, while continuing to refine how we deliver and support our services.</p>

<p>Over the three days, the summit will focus on:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Strategy sessions, product updates and partner collaboration</li>

<li>Direct access to Intermedia leadership and product teams</li>

<li>Practical insights that support future growth and development</li>
</ul>

<p>Events like the Intermedia Partner Summit play an important role in how we continue to improve what we do. They give us the insight, access and influence needed to make sure the solutions we deliver are aligned with both the technology roadmap and the real world needs of our customers.<br><br><br><strong><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-connectivity-orange-color">Adam Monaghan | Co-Founder &amp; Solutions Director</mark></strong></p>
