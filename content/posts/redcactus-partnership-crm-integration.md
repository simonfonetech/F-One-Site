<p>At F One, we’re always looking for ways to make business communication easier and more connected. One of the biggest frustrations for many teams is that their phone system and their customer database don’t talk to each other. That’s where Bubble&nbsp;comes in.</p>

<p>Bubble is a clever bit of software from our partners at redCactus that connects your existing CRM system, such as Zoho, HubSpot or Salesforce, directly to your cloud phone system. In simple terms, it brings your customer information and your phone together, so you can see exactly who’s calling and access their details instantly.</p>

<p><strong>How It Helps Your Team</strong></p>

<p>When a call comes in, a small pop-up appears on your screen showing who’s calling and key details from your CRM. No more scrambling to find the right record or asking customers to repeat information you already have. From that pop-up, you can:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Open the customer’s profile straight away</li>

<li>Add notes about the call while you’re speaking</li>

<li>See previous conversations or who last spoke with them</li>

<li>Transfer calls easily without losing information</li>
</ul>

<p>It works just as smoothly for outgoing calls too, simply click on a number in your CRM, and Bubble dials it for you automatically.</p>

<p><strong>Why Businesses Love It</strong></p>

<p>Bubble saves time, keeps information accurate, and helps your team sound more professional on every call. It also means:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Less switching between different systems</li>

<li>Faster responses for customers</li>

<li>Fewer mistakes and repeated questions</li>

<li>A complete history of every interaction in one place</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Easy to Set Up</strong></p>

<p>The best part is that it’s simple to install. You don’t need to be technical, we handle the setup for you and make sure it links perfectly with your phone system. Once it’s running, it just works in the background, quietly making your day-to-day communication smoother.</p>

<p><strong>The Result</strong></p>

<p>Your team becomes more efficient, customers feel better looked after, and every call is backed up with the right information at the right time.</p>

<p><strong>And it’s about to get even better...</strong>&nbsp;redCactus have just announced Bubble365, a new mobile app that brings the same great features to your smartphone. That means you’ll soon be able to access customer details, see call history, and take notes on the go, all from the same simple interface.</p>

<p>If you’d like to see how Bubble and the upcoming Bubble365 app could help your business, get in touch with us at F One and we’ll walk you through how easy it is to connect your CRM and phone system.</p>
