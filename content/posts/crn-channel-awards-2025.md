<p>I’m really pleased to share some exciting news as we come to the end of the year. At the CRN Channel Awards 2025, F One received a Judges Commended&nbsp;recognition in the MSP of the Year (Sub £10m)&nbsp;category, one of the most respected and competitive categories in the industry.</p>

<p>For us, this recognition means a lot. It reflects the people, the culture, and the care that sit at the heart of F One. As Lee New, our Co-Founder &amp; Sales Director said afterwards, “This one feels meaningful because it’s not tied to any single customer or project. It represents our everyday commitment, our values, and the standard of service we aim to deliver consistently across the board.”</p>

<p><strong>A Real Team Achievement</strong></p>

<p>Every part of the business contributed to this achievement. From the very first sales conversation, through smooth onboarding and installation, and into the ongoing support our customers rely on, this recognition belongs to the whole team.</p>

<p>Whether you’ve solved a complex issue, supported a customer through migration, built a bespoke solution, or helped a business grow with better technology, your work has played a direct part in getting us noticed on such a big stage.</p>

<p>Being Judges Commended is a clear nod to our customer first approach and the quality we deliver across every project. It isn’t about one moment or one department. It’s about the consistency, teamwork, and mindset we bring every day.</p>

<p><strong>A Moment Worth Celebrating Together</strong></p>

<p>To everyone at F One, well done. Your dedication, skill, and passion have been recognised by some of the most experienced names in our industry. This achievement reflects who we are as a team, and we should be proud of it.</p>

<p>Let’s take a moment to celebrate this together and look ahead to an even stronger year to come. Here’s to our progress, our teamwork, and everything we’ll achieve next.</p>

<p class="gb-text"><br><strong>Julian Gollogly | Marketing Coordinator</strong></p>
