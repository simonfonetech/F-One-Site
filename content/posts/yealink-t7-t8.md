<p>Last year, we spent time trialling Yealink’s new T7 and T8 series SIP phones, and it quickly became clear that these devices are set to become the go to choice for hybrid and flexible working environments as we move into 2026.</p>

<p>Designed to replace the older T4 and T5 ranges, the T7 and T8 series combine modern design, strong security, flexibility and a clear focus on sustainability. They are well suited to the way businesses now work, whether that’s in the office, remotely, or a mix of both.</p>

<p><br><strong>T7 Series: Everyday Business Work</strong></p>

<p>The T7 series is aimed squarely at general office users and reception environments. These phones offer a clean, modern design, colour displays and all the essential VoIP features most businesses rely on day to day.</p>

<p>Models such as the Yealink T74W&nbsp;strike a good balance between size and functionality, making them ideal for standard desk setups where reliability and ease of use are key.</p>

<p><br><strong>T8 Series: Premium Communication</strong></p>

<p>The T8 series is designed for power users, executives and high demand call centre environments where performance really matters.</p>

<p>These devices introduce advanced features such as AI powered noise cancellation, Wi Fi 6, Bluetooth 5.0, emergency buttons and even antimicrobial materials. The Yealink T87W, for example, combines a large touchscreen, excellent audio quality and extensive connectivity options to deliver a genuinely premium experience.</p>

<p>For businesses with heavy call volumes or senior users who want the best available desk phone experience, the T8 range really stands out.</p>

<p><br><strong>Smart, Secure and Sustainable</strong></p>

<p>Across both ranges, Yealink has clearly focused on three core pillars:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Smart workspaces</strong>: Clean, minimalist and intuitive designs that fit well into modern and hybrid offices.</li>

<li><strong>Secure foundations</strong>: Advanced protection across devices, networks and data to support today’s security expectations.</li>

<li><strong>Flexible connectivity</strong>: Support for Wi Fi 6, Bluetooth and USB C device mode, allowing desk phones to adapt easily to flexible working setups.</li>
</ul>

<p>Sustainability also plays an important role, with greener packaging, power saving modes and recycled materials used in the premium models.</p>

<p>As I often say to customers, businesses need tools that genuinely support flexible, secure and efficient working. The T7 and T8 series do exactly that, combining modern design with advanced features that align with how we work today.</p>
