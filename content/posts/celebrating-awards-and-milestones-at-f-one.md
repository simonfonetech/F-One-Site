<p>I’m absolutely delighted to share that we've recently taken home the 'Best Enterprise UC Customer Solution' award at the CNA Awards! This is a significant achievement for our company, and I couldn’t be prouder of the team who made this recognition possible.</p>

<p><strong>The Winning Solution for Angela Mortimer</strong></p>

<p>The award acknowledges our innovative cloud-based phone system for Angela Mortimer, a leading recruitment consultancy. They approached us seeking a flexible and efficient communications solution to streamline their operations across multiple locations worldwide, not just within the UK. Our tailored approach ticked all the boxes, featuring capabilities like call recording, intelligent call routing, and seamless integration with their existing CRM. The result? Their team is now fully equipped to stay connected, whether in the office, working remotely, or meeting clients on the go.</p>

<p>For Alex, Adam, and I, it’s always been about delivering solutions that don’t just meet expectations but genuinely make a difference in our customers’ businesses. That’s why this award is so special... it recognises the dedication and effort we put into going the extra mile for our clients.</p>

<p><strong>Celebrating Our 10th Anniversary at The Cricketers Inn</strong></p>

<p>Winning this award comes at a perfect time, as we recently marked our 10th anniversary with a memorable celebration at The Cricketers Inn. It was truly a day and night to remember (even if some of the details are a little fuzzy), filled with laughter, good company, and a deep sense of pride in our journey. From our humble beginnings as a start-up to becoming one of the UK's leading providers of cloud communication solutions, the road has been nothing short of extraordinary.</p>

<p>Reflecting on the past decade, I’m incredibly proud of the progress we’ve made. Our growth wouldn’t have been possible without the unwavering dedication of our team and the ongoing support of our customers and partners. You’ve all played a vital role in shaping our story.</p>

<p><strong>Looking Forward to the Next Chapter</strong></p>

<p>As we celebrate this milestone, I’d like to take a moment to thank everyone who has been part of our journey. There have certainly been challenges along the way, but the rewards have made it all worthwhile.</p>

<p>Looking forward, we have ambitious plans to continue pushing the boundaries of cloud communications, helping even more businesses stay connected and thrive. Here’s to the next ten years of growth, innovation, and delivering first-class telecom solutions. I’m excited about what lies ahead and can’t wait to see where the next chapter takes us.</p>

<p>Thank you for being a part of our story. There’s so much more to come.</p>

<p><br><strong>Lee New, Co-Founder &amp; Sales Director</strong></p>
