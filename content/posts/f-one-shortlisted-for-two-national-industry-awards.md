<p>I’m really proud to share that F One Technologies has been shortlisted for two prestigious national awards this year.</p>

<p>The first is the Comms National Awards, where we’re finalists in the <strong>Best SME UC Customer Solution</strong> category for the work we delivered with local company Radcliffe &amp; Co. This recognition means a lot, because even though not everyone in the team was directly involved in that specific project, it reflects the quality, dedication, and teamwork that runs right through our business, from sales and onboarding to installation and support. The awards ceremony takes place on Thursday 25th September at the Park Plaza, Westminster Bridge in London.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.cnawards.com/finalists"><strong><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-primary-color">CNA Awards</mark></strong></a><strong><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-primary-color"></mark></strong></p>

<p>We’ve also been shortlisted at the CRN Channel Awards 2025 in the highly competitive category of <strong>MSP of the Year 2025 (Sub £10m)</strong>. This one feels particularly special to me, because it’s not tied to one single project. Instead, it recognises who we are as a business and the service we deliver every single day. The ceremony will be held on Thursday 13th November at Battersea Evolution in London.</p>

<p><a href="https://event.channelweb.co.uk/crnchannelawards/en/page/2025-finalists"><strong><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-primary-color">CRN Awards</mark></strong></a></p>

<p>Whatever happens on the night, being named a finalist in categories of this calibre is already a huge achievement. It’s a real testament to the hard work and commitment of every single member of the F One team.</p>

<p>I’d like to say a big thank you to all of our colleagues, partners, and customers for your continued support. These nominations are something I feel genuinely proud of.</p>

<p><br><strong>Lee New | Co-Founder &amp; Sales Director</strong></p>
