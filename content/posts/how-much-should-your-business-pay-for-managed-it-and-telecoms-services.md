<p>Managed IT and telecoms services are now a critical part of running a modern business. But with pricing varying between providers, many organisations are asking whether they're paying the right amount and receiving the right level of support.</p>

<p>In 2026, UK businesses typically invest between £40 and £150+ per user, per month for managed IT and telecoms services. Most SMEs fall within the £55–£85 range, covering essential services such as proactive monitoring, cybersecurity protection, backups, and responsive helpdesk support.</p>

<p>Many businesses also bundle cloud phone systems and telecoms management into a single package, creating a more streamlined and cost-effective technology solution.</p>

<p>However, cost is only one part of the equation. As cyber threats increase and hybrid working becomes the norm, businesses need technology partners who can help them stay secure, productive, and prepared for growth.</p>
