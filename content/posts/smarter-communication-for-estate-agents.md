<p>At F One Technologies, I work closely with estate agencies and property management businesses who rely on fast, accurate communication to keep deals moving. That is why we partnered with RedCactus&nbsp;to deliver Bubble, a powerful integration that connects leading CRM platforms such as Street&nbsp;and Reapit&nbsp;directly with your cloud telephone system.</p>

<p>For estate agents, the CRM is the brain of the business. It holds everything. Landlords, tenants, applicants, property requirements, viewing history, notes, and next steps. Traditionally, phone systems have sat outside of that world, forcing agents to juggle screens, search for records mid call, or rely on memory.</p>

<p>Bubble removes that friction by tightly linking your phone system and CRM, so the right information is always in front of the agent at the right time.</p>

<p><br><strong>What This Means Day To Day For Your Team</strong></p>

<p>When a call comes into your business, whether an agent is working from the office, from home on a laptop, or purely from their mobile, the phone system checks the CRM in real time.</p>

<p>Before the agent even answers, they can see who is calling and key details pulled directly from the CRM, for example:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Is this a landlord, tenant, buyer, or applicant</li>

<li>Which properties they are linked to</li>

<li>What type of property they are looking for</li>

<li>Previous notes or recent activity</li>
</ul>

<p>This removes the awkward “Can I just pull your details up?” moment and allows the agent to answer already informed.</p>

<p>Once the call is answered, the correct CRM record can automatically open on screen. There is no searching, no guesswork, and no risk of pulling up the wrong client during a busy period.</p>

<p>From my perspective, this is where the real value starts to show. Estate agents live in their CRM, but telephony has historically sat outside of it. Bubble brings those two worlds together. Agents can answer calls already knowing who they are speaking to and why they might be calling. That saves time, reduces mistakes, and creates a much more professional experience for the customer.</p>

<p>The same simplicity applies when agents make outbound calls. Instead of copying and pasting numbers, or typing them manually into a handset or mobile, agents can click a phone number directly inside the CRM. The phone system then places the call automatically.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The agent never has to leave the CRM. That means:</li>

<li>Faster call handling</li>

<li>Fewer dialling errors</li>

<li>More time spent speaking to customers rather than managing tools</li>
</ul>

<p>For busy sales and lettings teams making dozens of calls each day, that difference quickly adds up.</p>

<p><br><strong>Every Call Logged Automatically</strong></p>

<p>One of the biggest challenges in estate agencies is keeping CRM records up to date, especially when teams are busy or working remotely. With Bubble, call activity can be automatically written back into the CRM, removing the reliance on manual note taking. This can include:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Time and date of the call</li>

<li>Call duration</li>

<li>Which agent handled the call</li>
</ul>

<p>For agencies that want to go further, next generation AI can also transcribe and summarise calls. That summary can be published directly into the CRM, giving other team members a clear understanding of what was discussed and what the next steps are. The result is a complete communication history inside the CRM, so anyone picking up the relationship can instantly see where things stand.</p>

<p><br><strong>Built Around How Estate Agents Actually Work</strong></p>

<p>No two agencies operate in exactly the same way, which is why Bubble is designed to adapt to your workflows rather than force change. Using the Automations module, agencies can create simple rules such as:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Automatically opening the correct CRM screen when a call starts</li>

<li>Prompting agents with call scripts or reminders</li>

<li>Ensuring key actions are captured during busy periods</li>
</ul>

<p>This helps standardise service levels, reduce admin, and ensure nothing slips through the cracks when call volumes are high.</p>

<p>Despite the power of the integration, setup is straightforward. While exact steps vary slightly depending on your CRM and phone system, the process is designed to be accessible to non technical users.</p>

<p>My team and I handle the guidance, configuration, and testing, ensuring a smooth rollout with minimal disruption to your day to day operations.</p>

<p>This integration is not about adding complexity. It is about making life easier for agents and ensuring customers get the right support quickly. When CRM and telephony work together, teams gain clarity, confidence, and efficiency on every call.</p>

<p>By bringing your phone system and CRM together, estate agencies gain better visibility, empower their teams, and deliver a more joined up customer experience.</p>
