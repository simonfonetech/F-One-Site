<p>I am <strong>DELIGHTED</strong> to announce that we’ve been nominated for not one… but <strong>TWO</strong> gongs at the&nbsp;Technology Reseller Awards&nbsp;2023!</p>

<p>Fellow Co-Founder Adam and I will be attending the awards event at the Royal Lancaster Hotel in London on Friday 25<sup>th</sup> May, where we have been shortlisted for the SMB MSP Reseller of the Year award and the MSP Install of the Year award.</p>

<p>We are extremely proud of the nominations and the recognition they have brought us as a team. This achievement highlights our commitment to developing high-quality telecom solutions that meet and surpass our customers’&nbsp;expectations.</p>

<p>Most importantly, I want to make it abundantly clear that I believe we owe all of our current and future success to the hard work and dedication of every single member of the F One team. Their creative thinking, relentless efforts, and consistent commitment to our customers satisfaction have helped us stand out in a very competitive industry. We are really appreciative of their contributions and cannot express how much we value their hard work and dedication. This accomplishment would not have been possible without them.</p>

<p><strong>The Technology Reseller Awards</strong></p>

<p>For those of you that don’t know, the Technology Reseller Awards are one of the most prominent events in the telecoms and IT industries, and being shortlisted among some of the most inventive and successful firms in our sector is an absolute honour.</p>

<p>The SMB MSP Reseller of the Year award acknowledges excellence in providing telecommunications and information technology solutions to small and medium-sized organisations. We understand the issues that small and medium-sized businesses experience while maintaining their communication infrastructure, and we continually design bespoke solutions to fit their specific requirements.</p>

<p>The MSP Install of the Year award recognises excellent installation projects that have generated exceptional results for customers. Our team has worked tirelessly to provide our clients with unique and effective solutions, and we are honoured to have been nominated for this award.</p>

<p>We would like to thank our customers for their continuous faith in us and for allowing us to provide them with the best communications solutions possible. We'd also want to thank the judges of the Technology Reseller Awards for considering our company for these important awards.</p>

<p>We are really pleased about F One's future and the prospects that lie ahead. We remain dedicated to offering the best solutions for our clients, and we look forward to attending the awards event and celebrating the industry's achievements.</p>

<p>Thank you everybody for your support. Updates will follow after the ceremony…</p>
