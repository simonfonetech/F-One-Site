<h1 class="gb-headline gb-headline-e83cf586 gb-headline-text">Call Queues &amp; Hunt Groups<mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-highligh-marketing-color">.</mark></h1>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-984adb6d gb-headline-text">Make sure every call reaches the right people, even when the right people are busy. Hunt groups ring a team rather than an individual, an auto attendant guides callers to the right department, and call queues hold callers with music or messages until someone is free. For busier teams, advanced hunt groups add agent log-in, wrap-up time, wallboards, reports and supervisor tools, all configured for you and easy to adjust as the business changes.</p>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-cf36339f gb-headline-text"><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-text-black-color">Never Miss a Call</mark><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">.</mark></h2>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-1d44253d gb-headline-text">Hunt groups are included on every Elevate plan and are the building block of good call handling: sales, support and reception each get a group, and calls ring the members of that group on their desk phones and apps until someone answers.</p>

<ul class="arch-list">
<li><strong>Hunt groups</strong> for each team or department, ringing members together or in turn.</li>
<li><strong>Auto attendant</strong> menus (&#8220;press 1 for sales&#8221;) that route callers straight to the right group.</li>
<li><strong>Opening hours, holidays and out-of-hours routing</strong> set once, so calls always go somewhere sensible: another group, a mobile or voicemail-to-email.</li>
<li><strong>Overflow</strong> to a second group or a colleague when nobody in the first group can answer.</li>
<li><strong>Voicemail to email</strong> with transcription, so a missed call is never a lost one.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-85c50525 gb-headline-text">Advanced Hunt Groups for Busy Teams<mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">.</mark></h2>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-a4f1c030 gb-headline-text">When a team takes more calls than it can answer at once, advanced hunt groups on Pro and Enterprise plans turn a ring group into a proper call queue with the tools a small contact centre needs, without a separate contact centre system.</p>

<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Queue Management</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li><strong>Call queuing</strong> with position announcements, hold music and greetings management.</li>
<li><strong>Agent log-in and log-out</strong> so only people who are available receive calls.</li>
<li><strong>Wrap-up time</strong> after each call, and configurable removal of agents who miss calls from the queue.</li>
<li><strong>Smart greetings</strong> and additional call recording storage.</li>
</ul>

<ul class="arch-list arch-light">

<li><strong>Scheduled and graphical reports</strong> on call volumes, answer rates and missed calls by group and by agent.</li>
<li><strong>Monitor, whisper and barge</strong> so supervisors can listen in, coach an agent privately or join a call.</li>
