<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-1d44253d gb-headline-text">Within minutes of a recorded call ending, a recap appears in the Elevate desktop and mobile apps and in the Archiving portal. No new app, no change to how your team works.</p>

<ul class="arch-list">
<li><strong>Transcription:</strong> a complete, searchable record of everything said during the call, so nothing is missed or misconstrued.</li>
<li><strong>Summary:</strong> a concise recap highlighting follow-ups and takeaways, saving time on note-taking after the call.</li>
<li><strong>Sentiment analysis:</strong> an instant read on the overall tone of the call, so you can quickly gauge how the conversation went.</li>
<li><strong>Topic detection:</strong> key subjects identified and grouped, making the most important points easy to find and review.</li>
</ul>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-a47160b0 gb-headline-text">After the call ends, the recording is automatically processed and a recap is generated within minutes, available in both the Elevate apps and the Archiving portal. Recaps are stored in the Archiving platform, with its access controls, encryption and retention options, so your call records stay compliant with legal and regulatory requirements.</p>
