<h1 class="gb-headline gb-headline-e83cf586 gb-headline-text">Sentiment &amp; Topic Analysis<mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-highligh-marketing-color">.</mark></h1>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-cf36339f gb-headline-text"><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-text-black-color">Read Every Call at a Glance</mark><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">.</mark></h2>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-1d44253d gb-headline-text">As part of AI Call Recap, each recorded call is automatically scored and tagged within minutes of it ending, alongside its transcript and summary in the Elevate apps and the Archiving portal.</p>

<ul class="arch-list">
<li><strong>Overall sentiment:</strong> an instant positive, neutral or negative read on the tone of the whole conversation.</li>
<li><strong>Topic detection:</strong> the key subjects raised are identified and grouped, so the most important points are easy to find and review.</li>
<li><strong>Summary and transcript:</strong> the context behind the score is one click away, with a searchable record of everything said.</li>
<li><strong>Searchable across calls:</strong> filter and search recaps in Archiving to find every conversation about a particular topic or with a particular tone.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-85c50525 gb-headline-text">Why It Matters<mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">.</mark></h2>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-a4f1c030 gb-headline-text">Sentiment and topics give you a way to understand hundreds of calls without listening to a single one. That understanding pays off in three places:</p>
<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-bab8c124"><div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-b51de444"><div class="gb-container gb-container-b51de444"><div class="gb-inside-container">
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Customer Experience</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li>Spot the conversations that went badly and follow them up before a frustrated customer becomes a lost one.</li>
<li>See which topics generate negative sentiment and fix the underlying cause, whether that is a process, a product or a script.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Coaching &amp; Quality</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li>Find good and bad examples quickly for coaching, and review how individual staff handle particular subjects.</li>
</ul>
</div></div></div><div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-1bad6562"><div class="gb-container gb-container-1bad6562"><div class="gb-inside-container">
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Business Insight</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li>Watch topics trend over time: a spike in calls about a particular product, delivery issue or billing query shows up long before it reaches a report.</li>
<li>Understand what customers actually ask about, and use it to shape training, FAQs, marketing and your auto attendant options.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Accountability</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li>When a call is disputed, the recap, sentiment and transcript give managers an objective account to work from rather than memory alone.</li>
</ul>
