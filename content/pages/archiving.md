<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-cf36339f gb-headline-text"><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-text-black-color">What is Archiving</mark><mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">?</mark></h2>

<ul class="arch-list">

<li>Powerful contextual, item-level search to quickly find and preview relevant interactions.</li>
<li>Retention policies enforced automatically, with no administrative or user action after the initial activation.</li>
<li>Single store, single pane: all of your company&#8217;s communications in one platform.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="gb-headline gb-headline-85c50525 gb-headline-text">Why Businesses Need Archiving<mark style="background-color:rgba(0, 0, 0, 0)" class="has-inline-color has-pink-color">.</mark></h2>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-a4f1c030 gb-headline-text">Effective communication is crucial, and the volume of interactions across channels keeps growing. Archiving lets you safeguard those conversations and get real business value from them, addressing three key needs:</p>
<div class="gb-grid-wrapper gb-grid-wrapper-bab8c124"><div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-b51de444"><div class="gb-container gb-container-b51de444"><div class="gb-inside-container">
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Productivity</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li><strong>Unified data insights:</strong> analyse conversations across every channel to uncover customer behaviour, employee performance and business trends, for better-informed decisions.</li>
<li><strong>Coaching &amp; quality assurance:</strong> managers can grade interactions, give feedback and lift frontline performance by reviewing employee and customer conversations in one place.</li>
<li><strong>Knowledge &amp; continuity:</strong> preserve and share communications so nothing is lost during staff turnover, leave or hand-overs, for faster resolutions and happier customers.</li>
</ul>
</div></div></div><div class="gb-grid-column gb-grid-column-1bad6562"><div class="gb-container gb-container-1bad6562"><div class="gb-inside-container">
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Mitigate Risk</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li><strong>Address disputes &amp; complaints:</strong> a direct record of every conversation removes the he-said-she-said, protecting your reputation and resolving disputes quickly.</li>
<li><strong>Satisfy legal obligations:</strong> with litigation, investigations and eDiscovery increasingly common, retained communications give courts, regulators and internal teams the evidence they need.</li>
</ul>
<h3 class="arch-sub arch-light">Compliance</h3>
<ul class="arch-list arch-light">
<li><strong>Adhere to regulations:</strong> industry rules (HIPAA, FINRA, PCI-DSS, SEC and others) dictate how interactions must be recorded and preserved. Automated capture into tamper-proof storage keeps you compliant and helps avoid costly fines.</li>
</ul>

<p class="gb-headline gb-headline-1fe8cfe8 gb-headline-text">Archiving integrates seamlessly with Elevate unified communications, contact centre, Microsoft Teams and email, and is designed to be deployed in minutes, giving you compliant retention of customer and employee communications without a separate system to manage.</p>

<li><strong>Data residency:</strong> complies with US, Canadian and European geographic location requirements.</li>
